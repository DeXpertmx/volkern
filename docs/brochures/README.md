# Volkern brochures

Brand and product collateral maintained in this folder. Each PDF is the canonical, in-tree copy — when you need to share a brochure with a prospect, partner, or candidate, pull it from here in preference to any email attachment still floating around.

All three PDFs are tagged as `??` (untracked) until the commit that introduces this folder, and are not yet in a previous git history. Sizes below are the files on disk at the time this README was added.

## `Volkern_Real_Estate_Brochure.pdf`

The original flagship brochure for the Volkern real-estate platform, sized at **321 KB**. Intended for first contact with prospective agencies, it introduces the company's positioning, the value proposition for an agency adopting Volkern, and the onboarding path. This is the version to use when sharing with leads who are still evaluating whether Volkern fits their operation; treat it as the legacy reference while v2 (below) is rolled out.

## `Volkern_Real_Estate_Module.pdf`

Module-level reference document, sized at **497 KB**. It walks through the operational modules that an agency uses day-to-day — CRM, inventory, communications, and the rest of the verticals exposed in the `hubs` / `vertical` tab of the mobile app — with feature descriptions and typical workflows. Best suited for an agency lead reviewing module adoption with their team, or for internal training sessions when new staff are onboarded onto the platform.

## `Volkern_Real_Estate_Brochure_v2.pdf`

Updated v2 of the flagship brochure, sized at **91 KB**. Condensed and visually refreshed, this version supersedes `Volkern_Real_Estate_Brochure.pdf` and is the one currently recommended for new outreach. At roughly a quarter of the size of the original, v2 is lighter to email and easier to print on demand; replace older copies of the brochure in circulation with this file.
